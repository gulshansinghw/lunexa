Child Safety Standards Policy (CSAE & CSAM Prevention)

App Name: Lunexa - Audio, Video & Connect
Last Updated: November 2026

Zero Tolerance for Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE): Lunexa maintains an absolute zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). Lunexa is strictly intended for adults aged 18 years and older.

1. Strict 18+ Age Eligibility

  • Access to Lunexa (as a User or Host) is strictly restricted to individuals aged 18 years and above.
  • Accounts suspected or confirmed to belong to minors under 18 are immediately terminated and removed.

2. Prohibited Content & Behavior

  • Any generation, upload, sharing, solicitation, grooming, or discussion involving CSAM or CSAE is strictly forbidden across profiles, chats, audio calls, and video calls.
  • Users and Hosts must also comply with our strict No Nudity Policy and Community Guidelines at all times.

3. In-App Reporting & Enforcement

Users can report any child safety concern directly inside the Lunexa app:

  • Go to the Account tab (or Host Profile) and select Report A Problem, or block the user immediately using Blocked & Hidden List.
  • All child safety reports are reviewed on a top-priority basis. Confirmed violations result in immediate content removal, permanent account termination without refund, and mandatory reporting to relevant regional and national law enforcement authorities (including NCMEC / cyber-crime authorities as required by law).

4. Legal Compliance

Lunexa complies with all applicable Indian and international child protection laws, including the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, the POCSO Act, 2012, and Google Play's Child Safety Standards Policy.
