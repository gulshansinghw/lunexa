Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Lunexa respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us while using the Lunexa mobile application and website.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account details such as name, email address, age, gender, and profile preferences provided during registration.
  • Transaction metadata required to credit prepaid talktime balances via authorized payment gateways.
  • Device and connection diagnostics to maintain call quality and platform safety.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide 1-on-1 audio, video, and text communication services.
  • To process talktime recharges, prevent fraud, and enforce community safety guidelines.
  • We do not sell or rent user personal data to third parties.

3. Audio & Video Privacy

All 1-on-1 calls are transmitted securely in real time. Screen recording and screenshots are restricted inside the application to protect user and host privacy.
